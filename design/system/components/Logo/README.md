# Logo

The Dockwell logo: the name set in Host Grotesk 650, with a geometric D whose counter holds still water. The D is the dock; the cobalt below its waterline is the well.

**You provide** a variant and a height. `logotype` is the default everywhere the product names itself; `mark` is the D alone, for tight square spaces; `icon` is the app-icon tile for favicons, home screens and app stores.

- Height is the cap height of the D, in pixels. In the rail and top bars use 14; on sign-in and empty states, 16 to 24.
- Ink follows `currentColor` and the water follows `var(--cobalt)`, so both themes work without a second asset. Use `mono` on photos, in print and on coloured fills.
- Keep clear space of one stem width (18% of the height) on every side. Never stretch it, outline it, add effects, recolour the water to a status colour, or put the logotype next to the word "Dockwell".
- Files for use outside the product live in `brand/`: logotype, mark and icon as SVG, in light, dark and one-colour versions.
