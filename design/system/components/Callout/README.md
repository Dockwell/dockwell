# Callout

An inline notice about the current page: what happened, what Dockwell already did, and what the reader can do next.

**You provide** `tone` (info, healthy, warning, critical), a short `title`, one or two sentences of body, and up to two `actions`.

- Lead with the fact ("ams-1-03 is down"), then the consequence, then the fix. Never apologize.
- Use at most one Callout per view. Several problems belong in an alerts list.
- The ground is the tone's soft token and the glyph carries the tone. There is no coloured left border.
